# 📜 Non-Inspiration of the Day - Your Daily Dose of Existential Dread

![Non-Inspiration Screenshot](https://postimg.cc/xXV24j2h)


Welcome to **Non-Inspiration of the Day**, the website that reminds you daily just how meaningless everything is.  
Tired of motivational quotes and positive vibes? Here, we serve nihilism on a silver platter.  

---

## 🚀 **About this project**
This project is a beautiful waste of time designed to display extremely depressing quotes from great thinkers.  
Because why search for light when you can comfortably drown in the darkness of human existence?  

### **🖥️ Inconveniences**
✔ Randomly displays a pessimistic quote to start your day the wrong way.  
✔ Ability to change the quote if you haven’t had enough misery yet.  
✔ A button to switch to English, because depression is universal.  

---

## 💀 **Technologies Used**
We used technologies to ensure that your depressive experience is smooth and optimized:  

- **HTML5** - Because even despair deserves semantic structure.  
- **CSS3** - With completely useless rainbow animations to *ompensate for the bleakness of the text.  
- **JavaScript (ES6)** - To dynamically generate nihilistic content and ruin your day in real-time.  
- **LocalStorage** - Because even your browser should remember your sadness.  
- **GitHub** - So this collective existential crisis **can be shared with the world.
